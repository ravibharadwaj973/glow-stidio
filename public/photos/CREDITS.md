# Photograph credits

Stock photographs from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), used here because Glow
Studio is a **fictional salon** built to demonstrate the product. That is the
one case where stock is the honest choice: nobody is being shown a room that
does not match the business.

A real salon using this site as a starting point should replace all of them
with photographs of their own room and their own people. A customer who walks
in and finds somewhere else has been misled before they sat down.
