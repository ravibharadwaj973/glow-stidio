# Gallery photographs

Drop the files here, then name them in `src/lib/gallery.ts`.

Nothing is picked up automatically, on purpose: every picture needs alt text
saying what is in it, and that has to be written by somebody who was there.

- **Landscape** crops to 4:3, **portrait** to 4:5. Say which in `shape`; the
  default is landscape.
- Around 1600px on the long edge is plenty. A 6MB photo straight off a phone
  costs a customer on mobile data several seconds and gains them nothing.
- Only the salon's own work. See the note at the top of `gallery.ts` for why
  that is a rule rather than a preference.
- Ask before publishing anybody's face, and keep the answer. A customer who
  agreed to a photograph of their colour did not necessarily agree to it being
  on a website.
